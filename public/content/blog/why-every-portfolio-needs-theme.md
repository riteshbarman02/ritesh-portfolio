## The Sea of Sameness

Scroll through 50 software engineering portfolios, and you'll see the exact same layout: a dark background, glowing header text, a standard about section, and a basic 3-column project grid. While clean, these portfolios fail to do the one thing that matters in a crowded job market: stay memorable.

By implementing a bold theme, you transform a resume list into a storytelling experience.

### The Comic Book Paradigm

This portfolio is themed around classic Marvel comic books. Why?
* Instant Recognition: Every reviewer immediately understands the visual references: dots, narrator blocks, bold primary color palettes.
* Playfulness: Sound effects show developer personality and creativity.
* Interactive Discovery: Navigation panels resemble turning pages, which encourages users to explore and spend more time on the site.

### Technical Balance

A thematic design doesn't mean sacrificing core usability. 

> Ensure contrast ratios are accessible according to WCAG standards and that content loads cleanly on mobile viewports. If 3D elements are too heavy for mobile, defer their loading or provide a simplified version.

When you blend visual novelty with solid engineering practices, you create a portfolio that doesn't just show what you built, but highlights who you are as a developer.
