# Escape Room 3D Game

A complete interactive 3D web application simulating an escape room environment. Players can look around, examine clues, click objects, and solve puzzles to unlock items and find their way out.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **3D Scene Rendering:** Rendered entirely inside the browser using Three.js WebGL bindings.
* **Raycasting Interactions:** Dynamic mouse raycasting to click and inspect specific 3D items.
* **Camera Animations:** Smooth camera pans and focal shifts when focusing on specific puzzles or locked doors.
* **Physics & Collisions:** Simple boundaries to keep the player inside the playroom.
